# Reporting & Analytics

## Standard Reports

### Current Throne Status
- Allocation by layer (with percentages and USD values)
- Throne Score of each position
- Current risk metrics (drawdown, concentration, etc.)

### Performance Attribution
Break down returns into:
- Yield generated
- Price appreciation / depreciation
- Points / airdrop value realized
- Gas and other costs

### Weekly / Monthly Throne Report
Structured summary suitable for sharing.

### Stress Test / What-If
Simulate scenarios such as:
- ETH -30% / -50%
- Major stable depeg
- Protocol exploit on largest position
- Sustained high gas environment

## Style Guidelines

Reports should be:
- Clear and structured
- Honest about risks and uncertainties
- Free of hype language
- Actionable when possible
