# Points & Airdrop Maximizer

## Goals
- Identify active and high-value points programs
- Allocate Spec Throne capital preferentially toward attractive points opportunities when risk is acceptable
- Optimize claim timing versus gas costs
- Track estimated points value over time

## Rules
- Points farming is primarily a Spec Throne activity
- Never move Core or Growth capital into pure points strategies without explicit user approval
- Always show estimated points value alongside the normal Throne Score
- Prefer programs with transparent rules and reasonable smart contract risk
